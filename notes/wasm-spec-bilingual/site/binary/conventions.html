Conventions¶

The binary format for WebAssembly modules is a dense linear encoding of their abstract syntax. [1]

The format is defined by an attribute grammar whose only terminal symbols are bytes. A byte sequence is a well-formed encoding of a module if and only if it is generated by the grammar.

Each production of this grammar has exactly one synthesized attribute: the abstract syntax that the respective byte sequence encodes. Thus, the attribute grammar implicitly defines a decoding function (i.e., a parsing function for the binary format).

Except for a few exceptions, the binary grammar closely mirrors the grammar of the abstract syntax.

Note

Some phrases of abstract syntax have multiple possible encodings in the binary format. For example, numbers may be encoded as if they had optional leading zeros. Implementations of decoders must support all possible alternatives; implementations of encoders can pick any allowed encoding.

The recommended extension for files containing WebAssembly modules in binary format is “\(\mathtt{.wasm}\)” and the recommended Media Type is “\(\mathtt{application/wasm}\)”.

[1]

Additional encoding layers – for example, introducing compression – may be defined on top of the basic representation defined here. However, such layers are outside the scope of the current specification.

Grammar¶

The following conventions are adopted in defining grammar rules for the binary format. They mirror the conventions used for abstract syntax. In order to distinguish symbols of the binary syntax from symbols of the abstract syntax, \({\mathtt{typewriter}}\) font is adopted for the former.

  • Terminal symbols are bytes expressed in hexadecimal notation: \(\mathtt{0x0F}\).

  • Nonterminal symbols are written in typewriter font: \({\href{../binary/types.html#binary-valtype}{\mathtt{valtype}}}\), \({\href{../binary/instructions.html#binary-instr}{\mathtt{instr}}}\).

  • \({B^{n}}\) is a sequence of \(n \geq 0\) iterations of \(B\).

  • \({B^\ast}\) is a possibly empty sequence of iterations of \(B\). (This is a shorthand for \({B^{n}}\) used where \(n\) is not relevant.)

  • \({B^?}\) is an optional occurrence of \(B\). (This is a shorthand for \({B^{n}}\) where \(n \leq 1\).)

  • \(x{:}B\) denotes the same language as the nonterminal \(B\), but also binds the variable \(x\) to the attribute synthesized for \(B\). A pattern may also be used instead of a variable, e.g., \(7{:}B\).

  • Productions are written \(\begin{array}[t]{@{}l@{}r@{~}r@{~}l@{}l@{}l@{}l@{}} & {\mathtt{sym}} & ::= & B_1 ~\Rightarrow~ A_1 ~~|~~ \ldots ~~|~~ B_n ~\Rightarrow~ A_n \\ \end{array}\), where each \(A_i\) is the attribute that is synthesized for \({\mathtt{sym}}\) in the given case, usually from attribute variables bound in \(B_i\).

  • Large productions may be split into multiple definitions, indicated by ending the first one with explicit ellipses, \(\begin{array}[t]{@{}l@{}r@{~}r@{~}l@{}l@{}l@{}l@{}} & {\mathtt{sym}} & ::= & B_1 \\ \end{array}\), and starting continuations with ellipses, \(\begin{array}[t]{@{}l@{}r@{~}r@{~}l@{}l@{}l@{}l@{}} & {\mathtt{sym}} & ::= & \dots ~~|~~ B_2 \\ \end{array}\).

  • Some productions are augmented by side conditions in parentheses, which restrict the applicability of the production. They provide a shorthand for a combinatorial expansion of the production into many separate cases.

  • If the same meta variable or non-terminal symbol appears multiple times in a production (in the syntax or in an attribute), then all those occurrences must have the same instantiation. (This is a shorthand for a side condition requiring multiple different variables to be equal.)

Note

For example, the binary grammar for number types is given as follows:

\[\begin{split}\begin{array}[t]{@{}l@{}rrl@{}l@{}l@{}l@{}} & {\href{../binary/types.html#binary-numtype}{\mathtt{numtype}}} & ::= & \mathtt{0x7C} & \quad\Rightarrow\quad{} & \href{../syntax/types.html#syntax-numtype}{\mathsf{f\scriptstyle64}} \\ & & | & \mathtt{0x7D} & \quad\Rightarrow\quad{} & \href{../syntax/types.html#syntax-numtype}{\mathsf{f\scriptstyle32}} \\ & & | & \mathtt{0x7E} & \quad\Rightarrow\quad{} & \href{../syntax/types.html#syntax-numtype}{\mathsf{i\scriptstyle64}} \\ & & | & \mathtt{0x7F} & \quad\Rightarrow\quad{} & \href{../syntax/types.html#syntax-numtype}{\mathsf{i\scriptstyle32}} \\ \end{array}\end{split}\]

Consequently, the byte \(\mathtt{0x7F}\) encodes the type \(\href{../syntax/types.html#syntax-numtype}{\mathsf{i\scriptstyle32}}\), \(\mathtt{0x7E}\) encodes the type \(\href{../syntax/types.html#syntax-numtype}{\mathsf{i\scriptstyle64}}\), and so forth. No other byte value is allowed as the encoding of a number type.

The binary grammar for limits is defined as follows:

\[\begin{split}\begin{array}[t]{@{}l@{}rrl@{}l@{}l@{}l@{}} & {\href{../binary/types.html#binary-limits}{\mathtt{limits}}} & ::= & \mathtt{0x00}~~n{:}{\href{../binary/values.html#binary-int}{\def\mathdef1499#1{{\mathtt{u}#1}}\mathdef1499{\mathtt{64}}}} & \quad\Rightarrow\quad{} & (\href{../syntax/types.html#syntax-numtype}{\mathsf{i\scriptstyle32}}, {}[ n \href{../syntax/types.html#syntax-limits}{\,{..}\,} \epsilon ]) \\ & & | & \mathtt{0x01}~~n{:}{\href{../binary/values.html#binary-int}{\def\mathdef1499#1{{\mathtt{u}#1}}\mathdef1499{\mathtt{64}}}}~~m{:}{\href{../binary/values.html#binary-int}{\def\mathdef1499#1{{\mathtt{u}#1}}\mathdef1499{\mathtt{64}}}} & \quad\Rightarrow\quad{} & (\href{../syntax/types.html#syntax-numtype}{\mathsf{i\scriptstyle32}}, {}[ n \href{../syntax/types.html#syntax-limits}{\,{..}\,} m ]) \\ & & | & \mathtt{0x04}~~n{:}{\href{../binary/values.html#binary-int}{\def\mathdef1499#1{{\mathtt{u}#1}}\mathdef1499{\mathtt{64}}}} & \quad\Rightarrow\quad{} & (\href{../syntax/types.html#syntax-numtype}{\mathsf{i\scriptstyle64}}, {}[ n \href{../syntax/types.html#syntax-limits}{\,{..}\,} \epsilon ]) \\ & & | & \mathtt{0x05}~~n{:}{\href{../binary/values.html#binary-int}{\def\mathdef1499#1{{\mathtt{u}#1}}\mathdef1499{\mathtt{64}}}}~~m{:}{\href{../binary/values.html#binary-int}{\def\mathdef1499#1{{\mathtt{u}#1}}\mathdef1499{\mathtt{64}}}} & \quad\Rightarrow\quad{} & (\href{../syntax/types.html#syntax-numtype}{\mathsf{i\scriptstyle64}}, {}[ n \href{../syntax/types.html#syntax-limits}{\,{..}\,} m ]) \\ \end{array}\end{split}\]

That is, a limits pair is encoded as either the byte \(\mathtt{0x00}\) followed by the encoding of a \({\href{../syntax/values.html#syntax-int}{\mathit{u\scriptstyle\kern-0.1em64}}}\) value, or the byte \(\mathtt{0x01}\) followed by two such encodings. The variables \(n\) and \(m\) name the attributes of the respective \({\href{../binary/values.html#binary-int}{\def\mathdef1499#1{{\mathtt{u}#1}}\mathdef1499{\mathtt{64}}}}\) nonterminals, which in this case are the actual unsigned integers those decode into. The attribute of the complete production then is the abstract syntax for the limit, expressed in terms of the former values.

Auxiliary Notation¶

When dealing with binary encodings the following notation is also used:

  • \(\epsilon\) denotes the empty byte sequence.

  • \(||{\mathtt{B}}||\) is the length of the byte sequence generated from the production \({\mathtt{B}}\) in a derivation.

Lists¶

Lists are encoded with their \({\href{../binary/values.html#binary-int}{\def\mathdef1498#1{{\mathtt{u}#1}}\mathdef1498{\mathtt{32}}}}\) length followed by the encoding of their element sequence.

\[\begin{split}\begin{array}[t]{@{}l@{}rrl@{}l@{}l@{}l@{}} & {\href{../binary/conventions.html#binary-list}{\mathtt{list}}}({\mathtt{X}}) & ::= & n{:}{\href{../binary/values.html#binary-int}{\def\mathdef1498#1{{\mathtt{u}#1}}\mathdef1498{\mathtt{32}}}}~~{({\mathit{el}}{:}{\mathtt{X}})^{n}} & \quad\Rightarrow\quad{} & {{\mathit{el}}^{n}} \\ \end{array}\end{split}\]

约定¶

那个 二进制格式 (单位:千美元) WebAssembly 模块 是一个密集的线性 编码 联合国 抽象语法. [1]

格式由 属性语法 其唯一的终端符号是 字节. A 字节序列 是一个很好的形状 编码 页:1 模块 如果并且只有在由 语法.

每个 产生式 这个 语法 具有一个精确的合成属性: 抽象语法 双方 字节序列 编码 因此, 属性语法 隐含定义 a 解码 函数 (即分析功能,用于 二进制格式).

除了几个例外,二进制 语法 近距离反射 语法 会 议 日 程 和 议 程 抽象语法.

说明

一些短语: 抽象语法 在 二进制格式。 。 。 例如,数字可能被编码为它们有可选的领先零. 解码器的实施必须支持所有可能的替代品; 执行编码器可以选择任何允许的 编码.

建议的文件扩展名 WebAssembly 模块 输入 二进制格式 “\(\mathtt{.wasm}\)“ 建议赔偿额 媒体类型 “\(\mathtt{application/wasm}\)”.

[1]

额外 编码 层——例如引入压缩——可以在此处定义的基本表示值之上定义。 然而,这种层层不属于目前的范围。 规范.

语法¶

以下是: 约定 用于定义 语法 规则 二进制格式。 。 。 他们照镜子 约定 用于 抽象语法。 。 。 为了区分二进制符号 语法 从编号 抽象语法, \({\mathtt{typewriter}}\) 为前者采用字体。

  • 终端符号是 字节 以十六进制符号表示: \(\mathtt{0x0F}\).

  • 非绝对符号用打字机字体写: \({\href{../binary/types.html#binary-valtype}{\mathtt{valtype}}}\), \({\href{../binary/instructions.html#binary-instr}{\mathtt{instr}}}\).

  • \({B^{n}}\) 是一个序列 \(n \geq 0\) 重复次数 \(B\).

  • \({B^\ast}\) 可能是重复的空序数 \(B\)。 。 。 (此简称为 \({B^{n}}\) 用于何处 \(n\) 无关。 )

  • \({B^?}\) 是可选发生的 \(B\)。 。 。 (此简称为 \({B^{n}}\) 地点 \(n \leq 1\).)

  • \(x{:}B\) 表示与非绝对语言相同的语言 \(B\),但也会绑定变量 \(x\) 的属性 \(B\)。 。 。 也可以使用一个图案代替变量,例如, \(7{:}B\).

  • 产生式 已写入 \(\begin{array}[t]{@{}l@{}r@{~}r@{~}l@{}l@{}l@{}l@{}} & {\mathtt{sym}} & ::= & B_1 ~\Rightarrow~ A_1 ~~|~~ \ldots ~~|~~ B_n ~\Rightarrow~ A_n \\ \end{array}\)时, \(A_i\) 是用于合成的属性 \({\mathtt{sym}}\) 在给定的情况下,通常从绑定在 \(B_i\).

  • 大块 产生式 可分为多个定义,以第一个定义结尾处有明确的椭圆表示, \(\begin{array}[t]{@{}l@{}r@{~}r@{~}l@{}l@{}l@{}l@{}} & {\mathtt{sym}} & ::= & B_1 \\ \end{array}\),然后用椭圆开始继续, \(\begin{array}[t]{@{}l@{}r@{~}r@{~}l@{}l@{}l@{}l@{}} & {\mathtt{sym}} & ::= & \dots ~~|~~ B_2 \\ \end{array}\).

  • 有点 产生式 括号内附加条件,限制适用 产生式它们为组合式扩大 产生式 在很多不同的情况下

  • 如果同一元变量或非绝对符号在a中多次出现 产生式 (单位:千美元) 语法 因此,所有这些事件都必须具有同样的即时性。 (这是一个侧式条件的缩写,要求多个不同的变量是相等的. )

说明

例如, 二进制 语法 (单位:千美元) 编号 类型 现将情况说明如下:

\[\begin{split}\begin{array}[t]{@{}l@{}rrl@{}l@{}l@{}l@{}} & {\href{../binary/types.html#binary-numtype}{\mathtt{numtype}}} & ::= & \mathtt{0x7C} & \quad\Rightarrow\quad{} & \href{../syntax/types.html#syntax-numtype}{\mathsf{f\scriptstyle64}} \\ & & | & \mathtt{0x7D} & \quad\Rightarrow\quad{} & \href{../syntax/types.html#syntax-numtype}{\mathsf{f\scriptstyle32}} \\ & & | & \mathtt{0x7E} & \quad\Rightarrow\quad{} & \href{../syntax/types.html#syntax-numtype}{\mathsf{i\scriptstyle64}} \\ & & | & \mathtt{0x7F} & \quad\Rightarrow\quad{} & \href{../syntax/types.html#syntax-numtype}{\mathsf{i\scriptstyle32}} \\ \end{array}\end{split}\]

因此,字节 \(\mathtt{0x7F}\) 编码类型 \(\href{../syntax/types.html#syntax-numtype}{\mathsf{i\scriptstyle32}}\), \(\mathtt{0x7E}\) 编码类型 \(\href{../syntax/types.html#syntax-numtype}{\mathsf{i\scriptstyle64}}\),还有其他的。 不允许有其他字节值作为 编码 数字类型。

那个 二进制 语法 (单位:千美元) 限制 定义如下:

\[\begin{split}\begin{array}[t]{@{}l@{}rrl@{}l@{}l@{}l@{}} & {\href{../binary/types.html#binary-limits}{\mathtt{limits}}} & ::= & \mathtt{0x00}~~n{:}{\href{../binary/values.html#binary-int}{\def\mathdef1499#1{{\mathtt{u}#1}}\mathdef1499{\mathtt{64}}}} & \quad\Rightarrow\quad{} & (\href{../syntax/types.html#syntax-numtype}{\mathsf{i\scriptstyle32}}, {}[ n \href{../syntax/types.html#syntax-limits}{\,{..}\,} \epsilon ]) \\ & & | & \mathtt{0x01}~~n{:}{\href{../binary/values.html#binary-int}{\def\mathdef1499#1{{\mathtt{u}#1}}\mathdef1499{\mathtt{64}}}}~~m{:}{\href{../binary/values.html#binary-int}{\def\mathdef1499#1{{\mathtt{u}#1}}\mathdef1499{\mathtt{64}}}} & \quad\Rightarrow\quad{} & (\href{../syntax/types.html#syntax-numtype}{\mathsf{i\scriptstyle32}}, {}[ n \href{../syntax/types.html#syntax-limits}{\,{..}\,} m ]) \\ & & | & \mathtt{0x04}~~n{:}{\href{../binary/values.html#binary-int}{\def\mathdef1499#1{{\mathtt{u}#1}}\mathdef1499{\mathtt{64}}}} & \quad\Rightarrow\quad{} & (\href{../syntax/types.html#syntax-numtype}{\mathsf{i\scriptstyle64}}, {}[ n \href{../syntax/types.html#syntax-limits}{\,{..}\,} \epsilon ]) \\ & & | & \mathtt{0x05}~~n{:}{\href{../binary/values.html#binary-int}{\def\mathdef1499#1{{\mathtt{u}#1}}\mathdef1499{\mathtt{64}}}}~~m{:}{\href{../binary/values.html#binary-int}{\def\mathdef1499#1{{\mathtt{u}#1}}\mathdef1499{\mathtt{64}}}} & \quad\Rightarrow\quad{} & (\href{../syntax/types.html#syntax-numtype}{\mathsf{i\scriptstyle64}}, {}[ n \href{../syntax/types.html#syntax-limits}{\,{..}\,} m ]) \\ \end{array}\end{split}\]

也就是说,限制对被编码为字节 \(\mathtt{0x00}\) 接下来是 编码 页:1 \({\href{../syntax/values.html#syntax-int}{\mathit{u\scriptstyle\kern-0.1em64}}}\) 数值, 或字节 \(\mathtt{0x01}\) 然后是两个这样的编码。 变量 \(n\) 和 \(m\) 名称 各自的属性 \({\href{../binary/values.html#binary-int}{\def\mathdef1499#1{{\mathtt{u}#1}}\mathdef1499{\mathtt{64}}}}\) 非定数,在这种情况下是实际的 未签名 整数 那些解码为。 完整的属性 产生式 那么这是 抽象语法 限值,以前者表示 值.

辅助标记¶

在处理二进制编码时,也使用下列标记:

  • \(\epsilon\) 表示空 字节序列.

  • \(||{\mathtt{B}}||\) 表示 字节序列 生成于 产生式 \({\mathtt{B}}\) 在一个推论中。
